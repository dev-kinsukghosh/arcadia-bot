- **Captive Pokemon Fight-Back Breakout Command (`/pokemon fightback`)**:
  - Implemented `/pokemon fightback` allowing captured server members to initiate an escape battle against their trainer's Pokéball containment.
  - Balanced high-difficulty breakout battle mini-game (`startCaptiveBreakoutBattle`):
    - **Reinforced Containment**: Pokéball matrix starts at 125 HP (vs 100 HP captive), requiring deliberate defensive play.
    - **Actions**: `[💥 Heavy Struggle]` (28–45 DMG to ball, 25% recoil of 18 DMG), `[⚡ Precision Burst]` (18–26 DMG), and `[🛡️ Iron Guard]` (absorbs 50% DMG, heals +10 HP, reflects 12–20 DMG).
    - **Counter-Shocks**: Pokéball containment discharges aggressive counter-surges (18–32 DMG) every round with a 25-second move timer.
    - **Breakout Victory**: If the captive shatters the Pokéball (0 HP), the capture record is deleted from `pokemon_captures` and they break free into the wild.
    - **Breakout Suppression**: If captive HP reaches 0 or times out, the escape fails, notifying both trainer and Pokémon, and applying a 3-minute exhaustion cooldown.
  - Integrated with `checkFightBackPermissions` to ensure captive users cannot initiate breakout in channels they cannot chat/battle in.

- **Pokemon Mini-game Channel Fight-Back Permissions Safeguard**:
  - Prevented trainers from initiating `/pokemon capture` on members in channels where the target lacks active fight-back permissions (`checkFightBackPermissions` in `src/utils/pokemonHelper.js`):
    - **Locked / Inaccessible Channels**: Checks `PermissionFlagsBits.ViewChannel`. If the target user cannot view the channel (e.g. staff rooms, locked rooms they are not in), capture is immediately blocked with ephemeral notice.
    - **View-Only Channels**: Checks `PermissionFlagsBits.SendMessages` (or `PermissionFlagsBits.SendMessagesInThreads` for threads). If the channel is read-only for the target member (announcements, rules, locked info channels), capture is blocked to ensure the target always has a fair chance to chat and battle.
    - **Timeout Safeguard**: Checks `targetMember.isCommunicationDisabled()`. Blocks capture attempts if the target is currently timed out and unable to interact with buttons.
  - Returns clear, ephemeral guidance (`MessageFlags.Ephemeral`) explaining why the target cannot be challenged in that specific channel.

- **Pokemon Mini-game Opt-In and Opt-Out Protection System**:
  - Implemented complete opt-in and opt-out support for server members to choose whether they can be captured as a Pokémon:
    - **Default Status (Opt-In)**: All server members are opted in by default, preserving original gameplay.
    - **Capture Immunity (Opt-Out)**: When a member opts out, other trainers can no longer challenge, ping, or capture them (`/pokemon capture user:<user>`). Attempted captures immediately abort with an informative ephemeral notice without pinging or notifying the opted-out user.

    - **Auto-Release on Opt-Out**: When a user opts out, they are automatically released from any trainer party currently holding them, ensuring they cannot be summoned into battles.
    - **Flexible Scoping (Server vs Global)**: Supports both per-server opt-out (`scope: server`, default) and global opt-out across all servers (`scope: global`).
  - **Database Persistence (`pokemon_opt_outs` Table)**:
